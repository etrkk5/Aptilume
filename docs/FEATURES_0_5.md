# Competency catalog management

HR manages the catalog from **Competency catalog**. Add a competency, edit definitions/owners/domain/family/department, archive it or restore it. Codes are unique and immutable after creation. Name, definition and all five level definitions are required.

Deleting archives the competency and preserves past forms/results. Required common competencies cannot be removed. An archived competency must be removed from future team/role targets or restored before new generation.

Catalog writes use revisions to reject stale updates. Periods retain snapshots of the catalog and scoring context; later edits do not rewrite historical evidence. Legacy periods are snapshotted before changing referenced content.

The starter catalog contains 42 competencies and 210 level definitions. Its imported content is data and retains its authored language; organizations can adapt names, owners and definitions. EN/TR switches the interface. L1–L5 catalog support does not establish automatic L4/L5 verification by the current written assessment.
