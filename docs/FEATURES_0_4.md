# Policies, response quality and filtered reporting

## HR-owned instructions

**Assessment policy** exposes question-generation, grading and insight instructions in one place. The default instructions are available in development, presentation and production. HR edits a draft and saves a new immutable version. Jobs pin the active version when queued; existing jobs/forms/results retain their versions. Data access, schemas, bounds, review gates and output-language contracts cannot be overridden by editable instructions.

## Required responses and quality review

Every assigned question is required. Empty/whitespace responses cannot be submitted. Honest lack of experience is valid and distinct from misconduct. After closure, LLM quality classification and bounded local pattern checks may flag nonsensical, off-topic, insufficient-content or instruction-attempt responses.

A flag is a provisional response-level observation, not a judgment about a person. HR sees evidence excerpts and records an explicit score/reason. A person cannot approve their own response. Pending quality cases block publication until resolved. Technical model failures are retried/failed jobs, not zero scores.

## Reporting

Dashboard and Excel apply the same authorized people and selected filters. Available dimensions include team, competency, role/title, section, job band and response status. Common written performance, knowledge checks, self-report, level coverage and measurable target gaps are distinct metrics.

Different role sets are not pooled into one success score. Comparisons use shared questions/rubrics. Cohorts with fewer than five valid people have aggregate scores suppressed. Missing/suppressed values are not zero. Target attainment is limited to measurable L1–L3 targets. Reports expose source/version and counts needed to interpret the denominator.

Development actions have owners, dates, status and notes. Only authorized scopes are visible. All-team selection is available when creating an assessment period.
